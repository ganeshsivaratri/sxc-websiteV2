Refund Policy

Please review the payment and refund conditions before registering for an SXC project-building program.

Registration Fee

The applicable registration fee for the SXC project-building program is ₹2,999.

The registration fee must be paid before participation is confirmed.

Non-Refundable Payments

Once payment has been completed, participants are not eligible to request or receive a refund.

This policy applies because project planning, team coordination, resource allocation, and project deadlines may begin after registration.

Withdrawal After the Project Begins

Participants cannot withdraw from the program after project work has commenced.

Participants are expected to remain committed to their assigned project and team responsibilities throughout the program.

Project Timeline Extension

The standard project-building duration is 45 days.

If additional time is required to complete a project, SXC may extend the project timeline without charging participants any additional fee.

A timeline extension does not create eligibility for a refund.

Before Making Payment

Participants are encouraged to carefully review the following information before making payment:

  • Program structure and duration
  • Participation expectations
  • Project responsibilities
  • Payment terms
  • Terms & Conditions

If you have questions or need clarification, contact the SXC team before making payment.

No Guaranteed Outcomes

Payment for participation does not guarantee employment, internships, revenue, startup formation, commercial success, or placement opportunities.

SXC provides a practical project-building experience. Project outcomes depend on team participation, individual contribution, collaboration, and project requirements.
